Tools

Five small tools that each do one thing. They run entirely in your browser — no files, tokens or passwords are ever sent anywhere.

⇄ CSV ↔ JSON Convert in either direction. Quoted fields, embedded newlines and unusual delimiters are handled properly. {} JWT decoder Read a token’s header, payload and claims, and check its signature against a secret. •• Password generator Random passwords or passphrases from your browser’s cryptographic randomness, with entropy and crack-time estimates. PDF merge Drop PDFs, drag to reorder, pick page ranges, download one file. QR generator Type anything and get a QR code. Choose the error-correction level, download PNG or SVG.

In the browser

Every tool runs as JavaScript on this device. Nothing you paste or drop in is uploaded.

No accounts

Nothing to sign up for and nothing kept between visits. Close the tab and it’s gone.

One file each

Each tool is a single HTML file. Save it and it keeps working with no connection at all.
